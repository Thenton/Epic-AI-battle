Epic AI Battle (EAIB) - README

Game Overview

Epic AI Battle (EAIB) is a turn-based, text-based PvP battle simulation where players assemble a team of three iconic characters from fiction to engage in strategic battles. The game features dynamic narration, AI-driven decision-making, and an adaptive battle system that ensures every fight is intense and unpredictable.

How to Play

1. Team Selection

Players choose three characters from a diverse roster, each with unique abilities.

Teams must assign a team captain and select an initial strategy:

Offensive – Aggressive, high-damage attacks.

Defensive – Focus on blocking and countering.

Balanced – A mix of offense and defense.


Players can name their team for added customization.


2. Battle Mechanics

Battles are turn-based, alternating between teams.

Each round, players choose from the following actions:

Attack – Strike an opponent with character-specific abilities.

Defend – Reduce incoming damage or counterattack.

Hold Ground – Maintain position for a strategic advantage.


Characters have cooldowns, synergy bonuses, and unique interactions based on their abilities.


3. AI Simulation & Narration

The AI simulates battles 1,000 times to determine probabilities and adapt strategies.

Each action is narrated dynamically, creating an engaging battle recap.

The game ensures battles are cinematic, with dramatic deaths and finishing moves.


4. Victory Conditions

The battle lasts 3 to 5 rounds depending on team performance.

A team wins when all three members of the opposing team are defeated.


Game Features

✔ Turn-Based Combat – Tactical decision-making with a deep strategy system.
✔ AI-Driven Battles – AI adapts to strategies and calculates battle outcomes.
✔ Dynamic Narration – Real-time, immersive storytelling for every battle.
✔ Diverse Roster – Characters from different universes with unique abilities.
✔ Multiple Game Modes – Play against another player (PvP) or AI (PvAI).
✔ Stat & Cooldown System – Balanced gameplay with strategic ability management.

Installation & Setup

Ensure Pydroid is installed for Python development.

Download and extract the game files.

Run the main script (eaib.py) to start the game.
